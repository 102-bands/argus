ARGUS LOGO KIT
==============
Colors:  Ink #0B0B0F   White #FFFFFF
Typeface: Poppins Medium (wordmark is outlined, no font needed)
Mark: rounded monoline, stroke 14, one continuous line through both brackets and the null-set slash.

SVG/        Vector masters: use these wherever possible (web, print, Figma)
PNG/logo/   Full logo, transparent and on solid backgrounds
PNG/icon/   Mark only, transparent, 16 to 1024 px
PNG/app-icon/  White mark on a black rounded square (iOS 180, Android 192/512, store 1024)
favicon.ico Black mark, 16/32/64 px

Use the black logo on light backgrounds and the white logo on dark ones.
Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the circle.
